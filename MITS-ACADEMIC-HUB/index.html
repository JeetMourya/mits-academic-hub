Before you start

  • Enter your enrollment as issued by MITS, for example BTIT24O1040. A zero typed in place of the letter O is corrected automatically.
  • Ex / Backlog results are available only for examination sessions that have been verified. If a session is not verified for your batch and semester, you will see a clear message, never a guessed result.
  • Results come from the official MITS IUMS portal. This utility does not modify university records.

How It Works

Five short steps from enrollment number to result.

  1. 1Enter your enrollment numberYour batch is detected for you.
  2. 2Select your semesterSemesters 1 to 8 for your batch.
  3. 3Select Regular or ExEach attempt is kept separate.
  4. 4Complete the CAPTCHAA simple on-page check.
  5. 5Fetch and view the resultGrades, SGPA and status from IUMS.

Important information
